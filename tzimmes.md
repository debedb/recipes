# Tzimmes (цимес)

Carrots and dried fruit stewed in honey and date syrup until syrupy. Sweet,
served warm or at room temperature with tea. Not the brisket braise - no meat
here.

Serves 5-6 as a sweet.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb / 900 g carrots, peeled, cut into 1/4 in / 5 mm coins (a little under
  2 lb was used; not weighed)
- 250 g (8.8 oz) pitted prunes, halved
- 200 g (7 oz) dried apricots, halved
- 100 g (3.5 oz) raisins
- 2 oz (57 g) date syrup
- 1.3 oz (37 g) honey
- 3 oz (85 g) coconut oil
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 1/2 cups water
- 1.2 oz (35 ml) lemon juice, squeezed

## Steps

1. Carrots, water, coconut oil and salt in a wide pot. Cover, simmer 15-20 min,
   until the carrots give to a knife but are not soft.
2. Add prunes, apricots, raisins, date syrup, honey, cinnamon. Simmer uncovered,
   stirring now and then, until the carrots are fully soft and the liquid has
   reduced to a syrup that coats them. Expect longer than the 25-30 min this
   started out calling for: there is a cup more water, and half again as much
   dried fruit giving up its own liquid. Go by the spoon, not the clock.
3. Watch the last 10 min. Once the liquid is mostly gone the syrup scorches
   fast. Carrots still firm and pot going dry: add a splash of water.
4. Off heat, stir in the lemon juice. Taste. More honey or date syrup if the
   fruit was sour; more lemon if it is cloying.

## Notes

- The carrots are the one quantity not recorded: a little under 2 lb, not
  weighed. That line is still the starting figure, not a measured one.
- Coconut oil stands in for the butter, which makes this parve in fact rather
  than parve-if-you-swap. It is not a neutral oil and it reads in the finished
  dish. The honey is what keeps it off the vegan list.
- The sweetening is split, not reduced: 2 oz date syrup plus 1.3 oz honey is
  3.3 oz against the 1/4 cup of honey this started with, about 3 oz at honey's
  density. Slightly more, redistributed, and the date syrup lands darker and
  less floral than honey on its own.
- Dried fruit went up across the board, 550 g against about 385 g. That is
  where the extra cup of water goes: half again as much fruit takes up more
  than the old quantities did.
- Lemon more than doubled, from the juice of half a lemon to 1.2 oz. Against
  that much dried fruit it is what keeps the pot from cloying.

## Serving

Warm or room temperature, small bowls, with tea. Chopped walnuts on top if you
want the crunch. Keeps a week refrigerated and is better on the second day.

## Sources

Traditional Ashkenazi sweet tzimmes. No single source. The quantities began as
a starting point; the fruit, sweeteners, fat, water and lemon have since been
corrected against an actual cook. The carrots have not.
