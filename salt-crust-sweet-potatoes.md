# Salt-Crust Sweet Potatoes

Whole sweet potatoes buried in salt and egg white, baked hard, cracked open at
the table. Serves 6 as a side. The crust also takes 3 lb (1.4 kg) of coarse
kosher salt and 4 egg whites, kept off the list because nobody eats them.

## Ingredients

- 6 small sweet potatoes, about 6 oz (170 g) each, skins unbroken
- 3 tbsp coconut oil, soft
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/8 tsp cayenne
- 1 lemon, cut in 6 wedges

## Steps

1. Oven to 425 F. Scrub and dry the sweet potatoes. A torn skin lets the salt
   in, so leave out any that are cut.
2. Whip 4 egg whites stiff. Fold in 3 lb (1.4 kg) coarse kosher salt until it
   packs like damp sand.
3. Press 1/2 in of it into a snug baking dish. Potatoes on top, not touching.
   Bury them and smooth it over.
4. Bake 1 to 1 1/4 h, until a skewer pushed through the crust finds the potato
   very tender.
5. Mash the coconut oil with the pepper, cinnamon and cayenne.
6. At the table, set the tip of a table knife on the crust about 1 in from a
   potato, tap it in with a mallet, and work around until the potato lifts free.
   Brush off all the loose salt.
7. Split, spiced oil in, lemon over. No more salt.

## Notes

- Salt, whites and oven are from salt-crust beets, 1 3/4 lb of them for about
  1 1/4 h. A baby sweet potato version uses about 1 lb of salt, 2 stiff whites
  and an hour.
