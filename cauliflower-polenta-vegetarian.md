# Cauliflower Polenta, Vegetarian

Vegetarian version of cauliflower-polenta.md. Chicken broth out, butternut
squash soup and water in; parmesan out, a jalapeno sheep cheese in. Sweeter and
hotter than the original, same texture. Serves 6 as a side.

Quantities below are what went in the pot, by scale, not what a source claimed.

## Ingredients

- 56.9 oz (1.61 kg) cauliflower florets, riced
- 8.4 oz (238 g) yellow onion, diced
- 3.3 oz (94 g) salted butter
- 0.7 oz (20 g) garlic, minced
- 10.4 oz (295 g) butternut squash soup, plain or creamy, dairy-free
- 12 fl oz (1 1/2 cups) water
- 5.3 oz (150 g) heavy whipping cream
- 3.8 oz (108 g) jalapeno sheep cheese, grated
- Black pepper
- Salt, only at the end

## Steps

1. Rice the cauliflower in a food processor, in batches, to couscous size.
2. Melt the butter in a wide pot over medium. Cook the onion 6-8 min until soft
   and starting to color. Add the garlic, 1 min.
3. Add the cauliflower, the squash soup and the water. Cover, simmer until
   falling-apart soft. Longer than the original's 15 min - there is more
   cauliflower and nearly twice the liquid. Go by the knife, not the clock.
4. Uncover. Cook down until the loose liquid is gone. Also longer than the
   original's 3-5 min, same reason. Do not skip: wet cauliflower makes soup,
   not polenta.
5. Blend smooth with an immersion blender, in the pot.
6. Off heat, stir in the cream and the cheese.
7. Now taste, then salt. The butter is salted and the soup carries about 665 mg
   sodium into the pot on its own; salting earlier oversalts it.
8. Too thin: back on low heat, stir until it mounds on a spoon. Too thick:
   splash of water.

## Changes from the original

- Chicken broth (1 1/2 cups) becomes butternut squash soup (10.4 oz) plus water
  (1 1/2 cups). This is the swap that makes the dish vegetarian. Two knock-on
  effects: the batch is sweeter, and total liquid goes from about 12 fl oz to
  about 22 fl oz, which is why both the simmer and the boil-off run long.
- Parmesan becomes jalapeno sheep cheese. Not only a flavor choice: authentic
  Parmigiano-Reggiano is made with calf rennet, so it is not vegetarian. Any
  hard cheese with a comparable salt-and-fat punch works, but see the note
  below.
- Butter goes from 3 tbsp to 3.3 oz, roughly double, and it was salted butter.
  Hence salting last.
- Cream goes from 1/2 cup to 5.3 oz, roughly 2/3 cup.

## Notes

- Rennet is the thing to actually check. The Forever Cheese page for Wooly
  Wooly Jalapeno lists pasteurized sheep milk from Castilla-La Mancha, Spain,
  and does not state the coagulant either way. If the cheese has to be
  vegetarian and not merely meat-free-adjacent, read the wheel's label or ask
  the counter for microbial rennet.
- Carbohydrate is no longer negligible. Pacific Foods lists 19 g carbohydrate
  and 10 g sugar per 1 cup serving; at 10.4 oz by weight, taking the soup as
  roughly 1 g/mL, that is about 1 1/4 cups, so about 23 g carbohydrate and
  about 12 g sugar into the whole batch - call it 4 g carbohydrate per serving
  across 6. Still keto-workable, but it is not the near-zero of the original.
- The soup brand matters. The one used is soy-based and vegan. A "creamy"
  squash soup made with actual cream is still vegetarian; one made with chicken
  stock is not. Read the carton.

## Serving

Same as the original: under braised mushrooms, roasted squash, or anything with
pan juices. The jalapeno means it no longer needs a hot element next to it.
