# Sweet Potato Soufflés in Orange Shells

Marshmallow-era orange cups rebuilt as real soufflés, with slow-roasted sweet
potato, coconut milk, whipped whites and no sugar. Serves 6-8 as a side, 12
shells. Roast the potatoes the day before.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb (900 g) orange-flesh sweet potatoes
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) orange juice, from 6 large oranges, shells kept
- 1 tbsp orange zest
- 4 eggs, separated
- 3/4 cup (180 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp lemon juice

## Steps

1. Day before. Oven to 275 F. Roast the sweet potatoes whole, unwrapped, on a
   foil-lined sheet, about 2 1/2 h, until very soft and caramelized underneath.
   Peel, puree smooth with the coconut oil. You want 2 1/2 cups. Refrigerate.
2. Zest an orange. Halve all six across the equator, juice them, scrape the
   shells clean without piercing them. Set them in a muffin tin so they stand
   up. Keep 1/4 cup of the juice here.
3. On the day. Oven to 375 F. Bring the puree to room temperature; cold, it
   knocks the air out of the whites. Whisk in the coconut milk, yolks, zest,
   orange juice, spices and salt.
4. Whip the whites with the lemon juice until stiff peaks just form. Stir a
   quarter into the base to loosen it, fold in the rest, stop while streaks
   still show.
5. Fill the shells to the rim. Bake about 20 min, looking from 15, until puffed
   and golden. Serve immediately: they fall.

## Notes

- The shells are Emeril's, with 3 lb of potatoes, a stick of butter, cream,
  brandy, 1/2 cup of brown sugar and whole eggs beaten straight in. Here the
  slow roast does the sugar's job.
- The whites are Williams-Sonoma's, 6 separated eggs to 2 1/2 cups of puree. The
  butter and milk sauce that version starts from is gone; the puree carries it.
  Its 375 F for 20-25 min is for 2-cup dishes, and a shell holds far less.
- Less sweet than the marshmallow kind, on purpose.
