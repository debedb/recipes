# Delicata Escabeche

Delicata rings fried in olive oil, then left overnight in hot spiced orange and
lemon. Serves 8 as a cold side. Start the day before.

## Ingredients

- 3 lb (1.4 kg) delicata squash, about 3, skin on
- 1/3 cup delicata seeds, from the squash
- 3/4 cup (180 ml) olive oil
- 1 cup (240 ml) orange juice, from about 3 oranges
- 4 strips orange peel
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) lemon juice, from about 3 lemons
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp cayenne
- 2 tsp kosher salt

## Steps

1. Cut the delicata crosswise into 1/3 in (8 mm) rings and push out the seeds.
   Rinse the seeds and dry them well.
2. Heat the oil in a wide skillet over medium-high. Fry the seeds, lid on, until
   golden; they jump. Lift out, salt, set aside.
3. Fry the rings in one layer, in batches, about 3 min on the first side and 2-3
   min on the second, until brown at the edges and easy to pierce. Layer them in
   a wide dish with 1 1/2 tsp of the salt.
4. Pour off all but 1/4 cup of the oil. Off heat, stir in the spices, wait a
   minute, then add the orange juice and peel. Hotter than that and it spits.
   Boil down by half. Off heat, add the lemon juice and the rest of the salt.
5. Pour it hot over the squash. Cool, cover, refrigerate overnight, turning
   once.
6. Bring to room temperature. Seeds on top just before serving.

## Notes

- The sources use vinegar, 1 1/4 cups of cider vinegar boiled down by half in
  one, 1/3 cup of red wine vinegar raw in the other. Lemon is the acid here and
  goes in off heat to stay sharp. Orange replaces the sugar.
- The skin stays on. It is edible and it holds the ring together.
- For Cronometer, weigh the oil that is poured off and count the difference.
