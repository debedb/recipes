# Kabocha Pie

Sweet potato and coconut custard set inside a whole kabocha, which is the dish
and the crust. Serves 8 as dessert. Start the day before.

## Ingredients

- 1 kabocha, about 2 1/2 lb (1.1 kg)
- 1 sweet potato, about 8 oz (225 g)
- 3 eggs
- 2/3 cup (160 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 1 cup (240 ml) orange juice, from about 3 oranges
- 1 tsp orange zest
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground ginger
- 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/8 tsp ground cloves
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt

## Steps

1. Oven to 275 F. Roast the sweet potato whole, unwrapped, on foil, 2 to 2 1/2
   h, until very soft. Peel and puree. You want 2/3 cup.
2. Boil the orange juice down to 2 tbsp. Watch the end, it burns. Cool.
3. Cut a lid around the kabocha's stem, wide enough for a spoon. If the rind
   fights the knife, pierce it and microwave 3 min first. Scoop out the seeds
   and strings and leave the walls alone.
4. Whisk the puree, coconut milk, eggs, orange syrup, zest, spices and salt.
   Strain. Pour into the kabocha to 1/2 in below the rim; it swells. Steam any
   extra in a cup.
5. Sit the kabocha in a bowl that fits it, or it will not come out in one piece.
   Steam over simmering, not boiling, water, its lid alongside, 1 to 1 1/2 h,
   topping up the water, until the middle of the custard reads 170 F and a
   skewer slides through the wall.
6. Cool to room temperature, refrigerate overnight. Cut into 8 wedges. The skin
   is edible.

## Notes

- No sugar. One version of the Thai original, sangkaya fak thong, takes 180 g of
  palm sugar to 4 eggs and 200 ml of coconut milk. The slow-roasted sweet potato
  and the boiled-down orange stand in for it, and it will be a lot less sweet.
- Sources steam the whole squash 20 to 45 min and none of them weighed it. The 1
  to 1 1/2 h here is an estimate for this size with a thicker custard. Go by the
  thermometer.
