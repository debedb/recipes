# Sweet Potato Pavé

Paper-thin sweet potato baked in coconut milk, pressed overnight into a brick,
cut and crisped. Serves 8 as a side. Start the day before.

## Ingredients

- 3 1/2 lb (1.6 kg) sweet potatoes, about 5 medium, two colors of flesh if you
  have them
- 1 1/2 cups (355 ml) full-fat coconut milk
- 5 tbsp (70 g) coconut oil
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 3/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp cayenne

## Steps

1. Day before. Oven to 350 F. Grease a 9x5 in loaf pan and line it with
   parchment, 2 in of overhang on the long sides.
2. Warm the coconut milk with 3 tbsp of the oil, the salt and the spices until
   the oil melts. Whisk.
3. Peel the sweet potatoes and slice them lengthwise on a mandoline, 1/16 in
   (1.5 mm). Trim to the pan. Turn every slice in the coconut milk.
4. Lay them flat in the pan, colors alternating, pressing down every few layers,
   to 1/2 in below the rim. Spoon in coconut milk until it just shows at the
   edges.
5. Cover with foil. Bake about 2 h, until a knife drops through the middle.
6. Cool 2 h. Set a second loaf pan on top, load it with full cans, refrigerate
   overnight, 8 h at least.
7. On the day. Lift out by the parchment, pour off any liquid, cut crosswise
   into 8 slabs. Back in the fridge 20 min.
8. Crisp the slabs cold, in the last of the oil, in a skillet over medium, cut
   sides down, until deep brown, turning once. Or brush with the oil and give
   them 6-10 min at 500 F on a sheet in the upper third. Sweet potato goes from
   brown to burnt fast.

## Notes

- Coconut milk is thinner than the heavy cream in the sources, so coconut oil
  goes in with it. The oil sets hard in the fridge, which should help a soft
  sweet potato cut clean. Cut cold, crisp cold.
- The two source cards share the pan, the oven and 1 1/2 cups of cream, but one
  calls for 1 3/4 lb of sweet potato and the other 3 3/4 lb. By volume the
  larger number is the one that fills the pan.
- The 500 F finish is the sources'. The skillet is not; it is there because the
  oven will be busy. Go by color.
