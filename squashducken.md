# Squashducken

Sweet potato inside a delicata inside a spaghetti squash, mortared with spiced
kabocha and sliced into rounds. Serves 6 as a centerpiece. Allow 4 hours, mostly
oven.

## Ingredients

- 1 spaghetti squash, about 4 lb (1.8 kg), the fattest you have
- 1 delicata squash, about 1 lb (450 g), the slimmest you have
- 1 sweet potato, about 8 oz (225 g), long and thin
- 1 lb (450 g) kabocha, a seeded wedge
- 1 egg
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Steps

1. Oven to 400 F. Cut both ends off the delicata and hollow it into a tube with
   a long spoon. Halve the spaghetti squash lengthwise and seed it. Rub all the
   cut squash with 1 tbsp of the oil.
2. Roast everything: delicata tube 20 min, until it just gives; spaghetti halves
   cut side down 15 min, only enough to let a spoon in; kabocha cut side down
   and the whole sweet potato 45-60 min, until a skewer meets nothing.
3. Scoop the kabocha and mash it with 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp of the salt and all the
   spices. It has to stand up on a spoon. If it slumps, stir it in a dry pan
   over medium heat until it does not. Let it drop to warm, then beat in the
   egg. Hot mash scrambles it.
4. Peel the sweet potato and carve it until it slides into the delicata. Mash
   the trimmings into the mortar. Smear mortar inside the tube, push the sweet
   potato through, pack the gaps.
5. Scrape flesh out of both spaghetti halves until they close around the
   delicata. Rub the hollows with the last of the oil and salt and line them
   with the rest of the mortar.
6. Lay the delicata in, close, tie with twine in four places, wrap in foil. Put
   it on a rimmed pan: it leaks.
7. Roast at 375 F, 1 h in the foil and about 30 min out of it, until a knife
   reaches the middle with little resistance and the middle reads 160 F. Parts
   that went in cold take longer.
8. Rest 20 min. Cut the twine and slice crosswise with a serrated knife into
   1 1/2 in rounds. The spaghetti shell holds the round together and is not
   eaten.

## Notes

- Untested as a whole. The nesting is ATBBQ's, which goes spaghetti squash,
  butternut, zucchini. Delicata, sweet potato and kabocha are new here.
- The delicata decides everything: the sweet potato is carved down to fit it and
  the spaghetti squash is scraped out to fit it. Measure before cutting.
- All three sources nest raw vegetables and judge the middle by a skewer or by
  touch. Here the inside goes in cooked and warm, so the last roast only has to
  weld it together and set the egg. The time sits between theirs (90 min bare at
  350 F, up to 2 h in foil at 400 F) and has not been measured on this.
- Kabocha because it mashes dry. Butternut works and needs longer in the dry
  pan.
