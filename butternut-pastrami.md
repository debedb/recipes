# Butternut Pastrami

Butternut neck, dry-cured, crusted in pepper and coriander, roasted low and
shaved cold. Serves 6 as a starter. Start two days before.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb (680 g) butternut neck, peeled weight, from 1 large butternut
- 0.4 oz (12 g) kosher salt, 1.75 percent of the squash
- 2 tbsp coarse black pepper
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground allspice
- 1/2 tsp ground ginger
- 1/4 tsp ground cloves
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

## Steps

1. Day one. Halve the neck lengthwise. Weigh it and reset the salt to 1.75
   percent if it is not 1 1/2 lb. Rub the salt all over, seal in a bag with the
   air pressed out, refrigerate overnight or up to 3 days.
2. Day two. Oven to 275 F. Pour off the liquid and pat dry. Do not rinse. Coat
   with 2 tbsp of the oil, mix the spices and press on all of them.
3. Roast on a sheet, uncovered, about 2 h. Start checking at 1 1/2 h. Done when
   a skewer goes through with some drag. Soft all the way through is too far:
   it will not slice.
4. Cool, wrap tight, refrigerate overnight. Warm, it falls apart under the
   knife.
5. Shave crosswise, 1/8 in (3 mm) or thinner. Dress with the last of the oil and
   the lemon juice. Serve cold or at room temperature.

## Notes

- Untested. No source found makes this from butternut. The cure is a dry cure
  for beets: 1.75 percent salt by weight, airtight, 2 to 3 days. Overnight is
  short, so the middle will be milder than the edge.
- The oven is a 275 F squash, quartered and roasted 2 1/2 to 3 h until
  collapsing, stopped early.
- The neck only, because it has no seed cavity. The bulb can replace the kabocha
  in squashducken.md.
