# Cauliflower Polenta

Keto / low-carb stand-in for polenta. Serves 5-6 as a side.

## Ingredients

- 2 large heads cauliflower (~3 lb / 1.4 kg florets), riced or chopped small
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 3 tbsp butter (or ghee)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup grated parmesan (~3 oz)
- Salt, black pepper

## Steps

1. Rice the cauliflower in a food processor, in batches, to couscous size.
2. Melt butter in a wide pot over medium. Cook onion 6-8 min until soft and
   starting to color. Add garlic, 1 min.
3. Add cauliflower and broth. Cover, simmer 15 min until falling-apart soft.
4. Uncover. Cook 3-5 min to boil off loose liquid. Do not skip: wet
   cauliflower makes soup, not polenta.
5. Blend smooth with an immersion blender, in the pot.
6. Off heat, stir in cream and parmesan. Salt and pepper to taste.
7. Too thin: back on low heat, stir until it mounds on a spoon. Too thick:
   splash of broth.

## Serving

Under short ribs, braised mushrooms, or sausage and peppers. Anything with
pan juices.
